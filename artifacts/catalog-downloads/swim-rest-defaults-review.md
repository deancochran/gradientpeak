# Static swim rest defaults

These are fixed, evidence-informed catalog prescriptions, not individually optimal or dynamically calculated recovery times. No pace assumptions or invented universal work:rest formula are used. Every timed passive swim rest is a positive multiple of five seconds.

| Template | Previous rest | Current rest | Coaching rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recovery Feel Swim | 25 s | 15 s | Brief reset during easy 200 m repetitions. |
| Tempo Pull Set | 35 s | 20 s | Shorter recovery preserves continuity during controlled 200 m pull repetitions. |
| CSS Threshold Swim | 40 s | 20 s | Short rest is consistent with common CSS/threshold practice. |
| VO2 Pace Swim | 55 s | 30 s | Practical incomplete-recovery starting point for hard 100 m repeats; not proof of an optimal VO2 stimulus. |
| Tempo Negative-Split Swim | 30 s | 25 s | Modest reduction for continuous controlled work over 250 m. |

Retained: 25 s for Aerobic Continuity Swim; 20 s for Sighting Endurance Swim; 70 s for Anaerobic 50s; 85 s for Neuromuscular 25s; 15 s for Critical Swim Speed (CSS); 20 s between assessment build swims. Longer speed recoveries preserve quality and are not shortened solely because the work distance is short. Assessment active recovery and all distance-based active recoveries remain unchanged.

Evidence anchor: Effortless Swimming's CSS guidance describes common 100 m/200 m threshold sets with 10–20 s recovery: https://effortlessswimming.com/css-calculator/ . This is coaching guidance, not a trial establishing the precise defaults above. The other adjustments are conservative coaching choices for these session purposes; distance and RPE alone cannot establish a scientifically exact recovery duration.

Rest means time stopped at the wall after completing the repetition, not a send-off interval. If prescribed effort cannot be maintained with controlled technique, reduce effort or stop the set rather than forcing deteriorating repetitions. No automatic extension or athlete-specific timing is added.

Scope: only five passive-rest durations changed, in both authored and effective fixed templates. Work distance, intensity, repeats, order, IDs, active recovery, and assessment protocols are preserved. A before/after comparison across all 68 effective templates confirmed this scope. The preservation digest was updated only after that comparison.
